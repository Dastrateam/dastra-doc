# Step 4: Prioritize actions

Once the record is compiled, you set about listing all the tasks to carry out to comply with the current and future obligations of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You then prioritize these actions in light of the risks your processing activities pose to the rights and freedoms of data subjects.

{% hint style="info" %}
Not sure where to start? The [CNIL's GDPR developer's guide](https://www.cnil.fr/en/gdpr-developers-guide) is a good place to start.
{% endhint %}

Once you have identified the actions required for your compliance project, you create the associated tasks and assign them to the relevant colleagues in the **Tasks** menu of Dastra:
