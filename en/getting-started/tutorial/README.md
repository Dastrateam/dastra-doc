In this tutorial, Dastra puts you in the shoes of a controller in charge of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance of their organization. Step by step, based on this scenario, you learn how to use Dastra's various features.

## Use case (fictional): the SHIPBUILDER company

In this scenario, you are the business manager of SHIPBUILDER, an organization that builds boats. It is made up of 2 legal entities and 8 departments:

* HR
* General Secretariat
* Production
* Marketing & Sales
* Security
* IT
* Legal
* Procurement

You have decided to bring your company into compliance with the GDPR. After [reading about](https://www.cnil.fr/en/sheet-ndeg0-develop-compliance-gdpr) the CNIL's approach to GDPR compliance, you start without delay.
