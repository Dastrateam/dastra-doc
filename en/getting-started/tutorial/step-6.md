# Step 6: Document compliance

To prove your compliance with the regulation, you must compile and consolidate the necessary documentation. The actions and documents produced at each step must be regularly reviewed and updated to ensure continuous data protection.

## Your compliance file must include in particular

| Category | Documentation |
| --- | --- |
| Documentation on your personal data processing | **Record of processing activities** (for controllers) or of categories of processing activities (for processors) |
| Documentation on your personal data processing | **Data protection impact assessments (DPIAs)** for processing likely to result in a high risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals |
| Documentation on your personal data processing | **The framework for data transfers** outside the European Union (in particular standard contractual clauses, BCRs and certifications) |
| Informing people | **Information notices** |
| Informing people | Templates for **obtaining the consent of data subjects** |
| Informing people | Procedures for **exercising rights** |
| Contracts that define the roles and responsibilities of the parties | **Contracts with processors** |
| Contracts that define the roles and responsibilities of the parties | Internal procedures **in the event of a data breach** |
| The rights of individuals and the exercise of their rights | Proof that data subjects **have given their consent** when the processing of their data is based on it |

If your documentation shows that you meet the obligations laid down by the European regulation, you have completed this step.
