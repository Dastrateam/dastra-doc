# Step 1: Setting up

First, you read up on the role of the DPO and on the requirements for appointing one, using the [CNIL practical guide on data protection officers](https://www.cnil.fr/sites/cnil/files/atoms/files/cnil-gdpr_practical_guide_data-protection-officers.pdf), then you decide whether to appoint a DPO in your organization.

Your choice is an employee (DPO or not) who, in turn, defines and leads a network of GDPR correspondents within the company's various businesses.

{% hint style="info" %}
The appointment of a data protection officer (DPO) is **mandatory** only for:

* public bodies;
* companies whose core activity involves regular and systematic large-scale monitoring of individuals, or large-scale processing of so-called "sensitive" data or data relating to criminal convictions and offenses.
{% endhint %}

In Dastra, you start by [creating your workspace](../setup/workspace.md) and [your organizational units](../setup/organizational-units.md), then you [add the DPO](../setup/dpo.md) to the relevant entity and [invite the members of your network](../setup/users.md).
