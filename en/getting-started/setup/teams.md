# Create and assign teams

In Dastra, users are grouped into teams. Teams are then assigned to legal entities or to departments, which lets you manage roles and responsibilities precisely.

## Create a team or add a user to a team

In the left menu, click **Workspace settings**, then **Teams**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/getting-started-teams-1.png" alt="List of teams in the workspace settings"><figcaption><p>The list of teams</p></figcaption></figure>

To create a team, click **Add a new team**, give it a **Name** and, if you want, an **Internal reference**.

To add a user to an existing team, click the three dots to the right of the team, then **Edit**, or click the team name directly. The list of users assigned to the team appears: add the users you want, or send an invitation to a new user.

To delete a team, click **Delete** in the team's menu.

## Assign a team to organizational units

A team gives access to the content of the organizational units it is assigned to. You can assign it in two ways.

* From the team: click **Edit**, then tick the units in the **Organizational units** list.
* From the organizational unit: in **Workspace settings** > **Organizational units**, click the three dots of the unit, then **Set permissions for**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/getting-started-teams-2.png" alt="Team edit window with the list of organizational units"><figcaption><p>Assigning a team to organizational units</p></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
As soon as an organizational unit has permissions, only the listed users and teams can access it. For the inheritance rules between parent and child units, see [Restrict access to the content of an organizational unit](organizational-units.md#restrict-access-to-the-content-of-an-organizational-unit).
{% endhint %}
