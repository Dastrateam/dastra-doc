# The customer support process

This page describes Dastra's customer support process: how requests are categorized, prioritized and handled, and how to follow your tickets.

## Support team availability

The support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm (GMT +2).

## Categories of requests

* **Bug reports**: description of an error or malfunction encountered in the application.
* **Troubleshooting**: difficulties encountered when using the application that require assistance or clarification.
* **Chat questions**: quick questions asked through the chat built into the application.
* **Other requests**: any other question or request about using the application.
* **Suggestions for new features**: proposals for changes to the application.

## Prioritization of requests

* **Bug reports**: they are considered urgent and handled first, to quickly resolve critical problems that directly affect the use of the application.
* **Other requests**: suggestions for new features, usage issues, chat questions and other requests are not prioritized. They are handled in order of arrival.

## Handling of requests

* **Bug reports**: the support team first tests the bug. If it is reproduced, it is escalated to the development team for correction. Once the fix is deployed, technical support contacts the customer to inform them and to make sure the problem is resolved on their side.
* **Other requests**: usage issues, chat questions and other requests are analyzed and handled directly by the support team.
* **Suggestions for new features**: they are forwarded to the product team, which analyzes each suggestion. As Dastra is SaaS software, the team does not build specific developments for each customer: it aims for improvements that benefit the whole community of users. Suggestions are prioritized according to their overall interest, the development time required and whether a simple workaround exists to achieve the same result.

## Ticket follow-up for customers

You can follow the progress of your tickets from the **Tickets** tab of the organization settings in Dastra. You see the updates of each ticket and whether it is being processed, resolved or awaiting other actions.

## Satisfaction survey

A satisfaction survey is sent 7 days after a request is closed.

## Continuous improvement

The team regularly analyzes feedback and trends to adapt the service and the product.
