# Questionnaires

Dastra's questionnaire module lets you collect the information you need to manage your compliance: DPIAs, processor questionnaires, compliance questionnaires, transfer impact assessments, and more.

To open the module, click **Questionnaires** in the left menu. The menu has three entries:

* **Questionnaire templates**: the templates you create, import or install from the library;
* **View questionnaires**: the responses to all questionnaires scheduled in the workspace;
* **My responses**: the questionnaires addressed to you, as a respondent or as an approver.
