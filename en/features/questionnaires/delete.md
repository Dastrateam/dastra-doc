# Delete a questionnaire or a template

In Dastra, a questionnaire or a questionnaire template goes through the recycle bin before it is deleted permanently. This step protects against accidental deletion: as long as the item is in the recycle bin, you can restore it.

## Delete a questionnaire

### Move to the recycle bin

1. In a list of questionnaires (**View questionnaires**, **My responses** or a template's dashboard), click the three dots of the row concerned.
2. Click **Move to recycle bin** and confirm.

You can also open the questionnaire and choose **Move to recycle bin** in the menu next to its main button. To process several questionnaires at once, select the rows and use the matching batch action.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/questionnaires-delete-1.png" alt="Row menu of the questionnaire list with the Move to recycle bin action"><figcaption><p>The Move to recycle bin action in a questionnaire's menu</p></figcaption></figure>

### Restore or delete permanently

1. Click **Filters** above the list, then turn on **View recycle bin**.
2. In the questionnaire's menu, click **Restore** to put it back in your lists, or **Delete** to delete it permanently.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/questionnaires-delete-2.png" alt="Filters of the questionnaire list with View recycle bin on and the Restore or Delete menu"><figcaption><p>The questionnaire recycle bin: Restore or Delete</p></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="danger" %}
Permanently deleting a questionnaire cannot be undone.
{% endhint %}

## Delete a template

Questionnaire templates have a recycle bin too.

1. In **Questionnaire templates**, click the three dots of the template.
2. Click **Move to recycle bin** and confirm.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/questionnaires-delete-3.png" alt="Questionnaire template menu with the Move to recycle bin action"><figcaption><p>The menu of a questionnaire template</p></figcaption></figure>

To restore a template or delete it permanently, turn on **View recycle bin** at the top of the template list. In the template's menu, click **Restore** or **Delete permanently**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/questionnaires-delete-4.png" alt="Questionnaire templates in the recycle bin with the Restore or Delete permanently menu"><figcaption><p>The questionnaire template recycle bin</p></figcaption></figure>

{% hint style="info" %}
The number of templates depends on your plan, and the template recycle bin counts towards this quota: a template moved to the recycle bin still occupies a slot until it is deleted permanently. If you want to keep a copy of a template before deleting it, export it as JSON from its menu: you can import it again later.
{% endhint %}
