# Assets

In the **Assets** tab, link to your document the assets it concerns: software, equipment, data or any other physical or intangible resource involved in carrying out the document. You track the resources covered by each contract or policy.

The assets come from your [data mapping](../mapping/README.md) referentials. To link one, click **Add asset**, select the asset in your referential, then click **Add**. The tab shows in brackets the number of linked assets.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/contracts-assets-1.png" alt="The Assets tab of a document with a linked asset and the Add asset button"><figcaption><p>Link assets to a document</p></figcaption></figure>

If the selected asset has an editor, Dastra points it out and offers to add it as a signer of the document: tick the box to add it together with the asset.

## Find documents from an asset

The documents linked to an asset also appear on the asset record, where the contracts button shows their number. From this button you can link an existing document with **Link to a document**, or create one with **Create a document**.
