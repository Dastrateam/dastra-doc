# View the processing activity tree

## Introduction

In Dastra, data can be visualized in several ways. One of them is the **processing activity tree**, organized by entities and departments.

This view shows in particular to which entities and departments your processing activities are attached.

## View the processing activity tree

To view the processing activity tree, go to the **Record of processing activities** module, click the arrow to the right of the **List view** button, then select **Tree view**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/ropa-tree-1.png" alt="List view button and its drop-down menu"><figcaption><p>Arrow on the right of the List view button &#x26; drop-down menu</p></figcaption></figure>

You can then **view the tree** of your processing activities.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/ropa-tree-2.png" alt="The processing activity tree"><figcaption><p>The processing activity tree in Dastra</p></figcaption></figure>

Each entity and department is shown with the number of processing activities attached to it. When they are filled in, its address and its representative are available from the help icon next to its name. Click the arrow at the start of a row to unfold an organizational unit and display its sub-units and processing activities, with their **Step**, **Type**, **Overall progression** and **Tags**.

Click a processing activity to display its details. Its action menu, displayed when you hover over the row, lets you edit, copy, export, share or move it to the recycle bin.

{% hint style="info" %}
The filters above the tree let you adjust the view to your needs. Users who manage the organizational units can also edit a unit or add a child unit from its action menu, and use **Create department** in the menu next to the **Create data processing** button.
{% endhint %}
