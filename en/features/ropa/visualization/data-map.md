# View the record data map

## Introduction

In Dastra, data can be visualized in several ways. One of them is the **data map** of the record of processing activities.

This view lets you explore, analyze and understand the links between the items of your record: processing activities, organizational units, stakeholders, assets, datasets, security measures, transfers, etc.

## View the record data map

To view the data map of your record, in the left-hand menu, click **ROPA**, then **Graphs**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/ropa-data-map-1.png" alt="Graphs entry in the ROPA menu"><figcaption><p>The Graphs entry of the ROPA menu</p></figcaption></figure>

You can then **view the data map of your record**.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/ropa-data-map-2.png" alt="Example of a record data map"><figcaption><p>Mapping example</p></figcaption></figure>

The toolbar above the map lets you:

* zoom in and out;
* restrict the map to some processing activities, to an organizational unit, or with filters;
* choose the **Types** of items to display;
* in the **Actions** menu, reset the zoom, switch to full screen, print the map, and turn on the **Dark mode**, the **Node labels** or the **Links legend**.

Click an item of the map to display its **Details** in the right-hand panel, with an **Edit** button to open it.

{% hint style="info" %}
Once the map is displayed, **double-click** an item to add it to the **Filtered nodes** and focus the map on it. The search bar of the **Filtered nodes** panel also lets you find an item and add it as a filter. Click **Reset filters** to display the whole map again.
{% endhint %}
