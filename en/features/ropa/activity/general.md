# General information

[**Article 30 of the GDPR**](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-30-gdpr/) requires each controller to keep a record of the processing activities under its responsibility. This record is the first element of "accountability" used to demonstrate compliance with the GDPR. The record of processing activities lets you list your data processing and gives you an overview of what you do with personal data.

The processing activity is the "umbrella" activity that covers all the purposes pursued within the framework of the activity.

For example, the "recruitment" activity could have two distinct purposes: the analysis of applications and the management of interviews, and the creation of a CV library.

The terms "processing activity" and "processing", often used interchangeably, can be understood in a similar way.

Article 4 of the GDPR defines processing as "_any operation or set of operations which is performed on personal data or on sets of personal data, whether or not by automated means_".

For example, it can be a database, an Excel spreadsheet, a video surveillance installation, a payment system by bank card or biometric recognition, a smartphone application, etc.

Processing of personal data may or may not be computerized.

[Read more](https://www.cnil.fr/en/record-processing-activities)

## Fill in the General section

The **General** section is the first section of a processing activity. It identifies the processing activity that you want to record in the register.

* **Name of the processing activity** (required, up to 120 characters): the name of the activity, which usually corresponds to the activity that involves one or more processing operations, such as customer management or event management.
* **Internal reference** (optional, up to 50 characters): your own identifier for this processing activity, if you have one.
* **Organizational unit** (required): the entity or department the processing activity belongs to. The data controller, its data protection officer and its representative shown in the **Parties involved** section are inherited from the entity of this organizational unit.
* **Tags**: classify the processing activity to find and filter it more easily. While no tag is selected, the AI assistant can suggest tags based on the name and description of the processing activity.
* **Short description of the processing activity** (optional, up to 3,000 characters): describe the processing activity. Click **Generate** under the field to let the AI assistant draft the description from the name of the processing activity, then check the result and click **Apply**. When the field already contains text, the button becomes **Continue writing content**.
* **State of the processing activity** (optional): select the current state of the processing in your organization: **To study**, **Being deployed**, **In production** or **Stopped**.
* **Deployment date** (optional): available once a state is selected.
