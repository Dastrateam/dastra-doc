# Manage cookies consent

## What are cookies?

[Cookies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HTTP_cookie) are small files that store information about a user visiting a website, for example their browsing history, site settings, etc.

The French data protection authority (CNIL) defines a cookie as follows:

> A cookie is a series of information, usually small and identified by name, which can be transmitted to your browser by a website on which you connect. Your web browser will keep it for a certain period of time, and send it back to the web server each time you reconnect to it.

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which applied from May 25, 2018, reinforced the requirements for valid consent set out in [article 82 of the French "Informatique et Libertés" law](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/la-loi-informatique-et-libertes#article82) (in French), which transposes Directive 2002/58/EC on privacy and electronic communications ("ePrivacy"). It requires, except in specific cases, that you collect the consent of users before any operation of writing or reading cookies and other trackers.

In early 2020, the CNIL published [recommendations on cookies and other trackers](https://www.dastra.eu/fr/article/recommendations-CNIL-cookies-expliquees-a-votre-boss/104) (article in French).

Compliance with cookie consent can be ensured by a cookie consent banner.
