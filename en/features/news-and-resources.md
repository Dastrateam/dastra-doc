# News and resources

The **News & resources** module brings together what helps you keep your compliance up to date: news selected by Dastra, your own RSS feeds, practical guides and templates, and the Dastra webinars. It is for every user whose role has access to the content.

To open it, click **News & resources** in the left menu. The menu has five entries: **Overview**, **Latest news**, **Rss feeds**, **Resources** and **Webinars**.

## Overview

The **Overview** page opens on a search field, **Search resources**, that looks for articles, practical guides and templates. The categories are listed under the field. Without a search, the page shows the **Last articles** and the **Last resources**.

## Latest news

**Latest news** lists the articles published by Dastra. Choose a category to narrow the list. Click an article to read it. In the article, **Copy the article url** copies its address so that you can share it, and **View article** opens the full article.

Some articles are compiled with the help of AI from public news sources. They end with a notice saying so. Check the source before you rely on one of them for a compliance decision.

## RSS feeds

The **Rss feeds** page shows the news of the feeds you follow, in addition to the articles of Dastra. Select a feed in the list on the left to read its latest articles. The feeds you added are under **Your RSS feeds**. The feeds shared by an administrator with the workspace are under **Shared news feed**.

### Add feeds

1. Click **Add RSS feed**. On an empty page, click **Create your first RSS feed**.
2. Select one or several feeds from the predefined list. You can search the list. A feed already in your list carries the **Already added** badge.
3. Click **Add the selected feeds**.

To follow a feed that is not in the list, click **Custom rss feed**, type the address in **RSS feed url**, then click **Check the RSS feed**. Dastra checks the address and reads the feed. Then enter a **Name** and a **Summary**, and click **Save and exit**.

Users with the advanced permission can share feeds with all users of the workspace. When adding feeds from the list, turn on **Share the feeds you add with all users of the workspace**. For a custom feed, turn on **Share this news feed with other users of your workspace**.

If a feed can't be read, Dastra says that its address is unreachable or no longer returns a valid RSS feed. Check the address with the publisher of the feed.

### Manage feeds

Click **Manage feeds**, or open **Workspace settings** > **Rss feeds**, to see all the feeds of the workspace. This page requires the write permission on advanced settings. For each feed you can **Edit**, **Delete**, **Share with the workspace** or **Stop sharing**, and **Show news** to read its articles. A feed is marked **Private** or **Shared**.

## Resources

**Resources** gathers the practical guides, templates and documents published by Dastra, by category. Browse the categories or use the search field of the **Overview** page. Open a resource to read it or preview it, then click **Download** to download its file. A resource shows its number of **Downloads**.

To use the document templates in your own documents, see [Document templates](files/templates.md).

## Webinars

**Webinars** lists the live sessions hosted by Dastra experts. Choose a category on the left, or **All**. Click a webinar to open its registration page in a new tab. When no session is scheduled, the page says **No webinar scheduled soon.**
