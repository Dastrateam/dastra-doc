# Customize the task workflow

Each task follows a workflow: it moves from one step to the next until it is closed. The steps are the columns of the **Columns** view. You can adapt them to your organization's tracking rules.

## The default steps

A new workspace comes with four steps. Each one is linked to an internal status, **New**, **Active** or **Closed**, which does not depend on the name of the step:

| Step | Internal status |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | **New** |
| **Info needed** | **Active** |
| **In progress** | **Active** |
| **Done** | **Closed** |

The last step, **Done**, is the final step: a task that reaches it is considered finished.

## Edit the steps

1. In the side menu, click **Workspace settings**, then, in the **Process** section, **Workflow stages**. You can also click, on the **Tasks** page, the arrow of the **Create task** button, then **Configure columns**.
2. In the list at the top of the **Workflow management** page, choose the **Tasks** object type.
3. Add, rename or reorder the steps, choose their internal status, then click **Save changes**.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/tasks-workflow-1.png" alt="The Workflow management page with the four steps of the task workflow and their internal status"><figcaption><p>The steps of the task workflow</p></figcaption></figure>

This page is reserved for users who are allowed to manage advanced settings. The detailed procedure (adding, ordering and deleting a step) is described in [Workflow stages](../settings/workflow-stages.md). You can also rename a step directly in the header of its column, in the **Columns** view.

## Classify tasks with tags

The **Tags** field of the task panel lets you classify tasks to find them faster: the **Filter by tags** filter uses them in every view. Tags are managed in **Workspace settings**, **Process** section, **Tags** entry.
