# Risk management

Privacy, strategic, compliance, operational, financial and vendor risks: the risk management module brings them together in a single repository. It is for DPOs, compliance teams and risk managers.

It gives you a global view of your risks to feed your remediation plans, extend your coverage and reduce your exposure.

## Risk management in Dastra in a few words

The module lets you identify, assess, treat and monitor your risks in one place, with an overview of your organization's exposure.

With Dastra, you can:

* view your risks by organizational unit, processing activity, asset, stakeholder or AI system;
* centralize the assessment, control, reporting and follow-up of remediation tasks;
* keep risk data up to date, reliable and available to the whole team.

## Attach a risk

In Dastra, a risk is attached to one of the following objects:

* organizational unit;
* processing activity;
* stakeholder, for example a processor;
* asset;
* AI system.

The object types you see depend on the modules enabled in your workspace. You can assess the same risk type on as many objects as needed.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/risks-list-1.png" alt="List of the risks of the Processing activity tab, with initial, current and residual risks"><figcaption><p>Example of consolidated risk view</p></figcaption></figure>

## The module at a glance

Open **Risk management** in the sidebar. The module is visible with read access to risks; creating and editing requires write access.

| Menu entry | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The indicators (initial, current and residual risk, remediation), the heatmap, the riskiest items and the latest control points. |
| **Risks** | The assessed risks, with one tab per object type. You create them one by one or in batch. |
| **Type of risks** | The repository of risk types: dreaded event, threats, sources, control points and pre-assessment. |
| **Threats** | The repository of threats, which you reuse across risk types. |
| **Sources** | The repository of risk sources. |
| **Control points** | The measures that reduce the risk, with their successive responses. |
| **Events** | The history of events that occurred in the organization, with their date and financial impact. |
| **Reporting** | The initial, current and residual scores per object (minimum, average, maximum), with one tab per object type. |

### Risk states

Every risk goes through five steps, shown at the top of its page: **Identification**, **Assess**, **Processing**, **Monitoring** and **Archive**. Each one is described in [the risk management process](process/README.md).

{% hint style="info" %}
Only the risk owner can change the state of a risk. The owner is shown in the **Identification** section of the risk.
{% endhint %}

### Create several risks at once

In a tab of **Risks**, open the menu of the **Create a risk** button and click **Batch risk identification**. Choose the organizational unit, the type of risk, the risk owner, the identification date and the objects concerned: Dastra creates one risk per object, with the pre-assessment of the risk type.
