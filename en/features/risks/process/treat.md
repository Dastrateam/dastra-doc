# 3. Treat

The treatment step is the response given to the identified and assessed risk. In the risk page, it is the **Risk response** section.

Choose an option in the **Risk response type** field:

* **Mitigation and controls**: measures must be implemented to reduce the risk;
* **Acceptance**: the organization accepts the raw risk as it stands, without reduction measures;
* **Avoidance**: the risk prevents the activity in question from being carried out;
* **Transferance**: the risk weighing on the organization is transferred to another organization, for example through insurance;
* **Take risks**: the risk should be subject to reduction measures, but the organization agrees not to implement them.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/risks-treat-1.png" alt="Risk response section with the five response options and the response justification"><figcaption><p>The Risk response section</p></figcaption></figure>

Explain your choice in **Response justification**, then click **Validate response** to move to the **Monitoring** step. This decision belongs to the risk owner: only the owner can validate the response.

{% hint style="info" %}
Only the **Mitigation and controls** option relies on control points and on the remediation rate, which move the current risk. For the other options, Dastra does not calculate a remediation rate.
{% endhint %}
