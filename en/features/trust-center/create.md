# Create a Trust Center

This page explains how to create a Trust Center. Creation asks for a name and an organizational unit; you configure everything else on the editing page.

## Create the Trust Center

1. In the side navigation bar, click **Trust Center**.
2. Click **Create a Trust Center**.
3. In the window that opens, fill in the **Trust Center label** and the **Entity**. Both fields are required.
4. Click **Create a Trust Center** to confirm.

The **Create a Trust Center** button is disabled when you have reached the Trust Center quota of your plan. The quota in use is displayed at the top of the list.

The **Entity** field holds the organizational unit of the Trust Center. This unit determines the data that the Trust Center publishes. For the details of its effect, see [Homepage and general configuration](configuration/home.md).

## After the creation

Once the Trust Center is created, you are redirected to its editing page. It is a draft: nobody can access it until you publish it.

The homepage, questionnaires, data subject requests, record of processing activities, attachments, organizational chart and contacts are checked by default. AI systems and the compliance project are not.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/trust-center-create-3.png" alt="Editing page of a new Trust Center"><figcaption><p>The Trust Center editing page</p></figcaption></figure>

To configure and publish your Trust Center, see [Configure your Trust Center](configuration/README.md).
