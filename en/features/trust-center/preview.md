# Preview and share your Trust Center

This page explains how to display your Trust Center as your visitors see it, then how to share it by link or embed it in your website.

## Preview your Trust Center

Click **View**, at the top right of the editing page. The public portal opens in a new tab.

The button is disabled until the Trust Center is published. To publish it, see [Configure your Trust Center](configuration/README.md#publish-and-cancel-publication).

If you change the configuration, save your changes, then refresh the portal tab to see them.

## Share your Trust Center

Once the configuration is complete, you can share the URL of your Trust Center:

* copy the URL of the portal tab;
* or click the arrow of the **Save changes** button, at the top right of the editing page, then **Copy link**.

In the list of Trust Centers, **Copy link** is in the menu of the Trust Center's row.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/trust-center-preview-1.png" alt="Menu of the Save changes button with the Copy link, embed and Delete options"><figcaption><p>The sharing options in the menu of the Save changes button</p></figcaption></figure>

**Copy link** and **embed** are only enabled when the Trust Center is published.

The rules of the [Security](configuration/security.md) page, such as mandatory authentication or IP address filtering, also apply to visitors who use the link.

## Embed your Trust Center in your website

Click the arrow of the **Save changes** button, then **embed**. The **Embed the Trust Center** window offers two display types, to choose in **Display type**:

* **Page (with Url)**: the window shows the URL of the Trust Center. Copy it and share it, or point a link of your website to it.
* **Embedded in an iframe**: the window shows a code snippet. Click **Copy**, then paste the HTML part where the Trust Center must appear on your page. The provided script automatically adjusts the size of the iframe to the size of the Trust Center.
