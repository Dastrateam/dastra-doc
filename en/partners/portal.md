# Partner portal

As a partner, you have access to a partner portal. It lets you:

* view all your clients and the state of their subscription;
* ask Dastra to add a client, or to upgrade, renew or terminate their subscription;
* set up a template library for your clients;
* present your business on a partner page of the Dastra website.

If you have access to a client's organization, you can also open it directly from the portal.

## Access the partner portal

In your partner organization, click your name at the top right, then **Show partner portal**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Prerequisite: you must be the owner of the partner organization to display the portal.
{% endhint %}

The top of the portal shows the number of **Clients**, the number of **Requests** and the **Profile page** card. The list of your clients is below.

## Follow your clients

Each client is a card that shows its name, its plan, the number of users and entities against the quotas, and a marker when a template library is installed. The icon next to the plan shows whether the subscription is valid; hover over it to see the subscription dates. A warning icon appears when the subscription expires in less than 30 days.

The ⋮ menu of the card offers:

* **Access client workspace**: opens the client's organization in a new tab. The entry is disabled (**No access rights on client**) if you do not have access to that organization.
* **Subscription details** and **View bills**: open the subscription and billing pages of the client, with the same access control.
* **Update client**: see [Change a client's subscription](#change-a-clients-subscription).
* **Configure a source template workspace**: see [Set up a template library](#set-up-a-template-library).

## Add a client

Click **Add client** above the list, then fill in the form:

* **Name of the client organization**.
* The client contact (name, email, address, city, zip code) and the **Client country**.
* **Desired plan**, **Desired subscription start date** and **Desired subscription duration**.
* **Desired number of entities** and **Number of employees**.
* **Company registration number**, and proof of the size of the client company to attach.

Click **Send**. A Dastra sales representative contacts you to finalize the creation of the client.

## Change a client's subscription

In the ⋮ menu of the client card, click **Update client**, then choose the action to perform:

* **Update client**
* **Renew subscription**
* **Terminate client subscription**

Complete the form, then click **Send**. As for a new client, the change goes through a request handled by the Dastra sales team.

## Follow your requests

Click **View requests** in the **Requests** card to open the list of your requests, with their type and state. Click a request to see its details.

## Set up a template library

You can give the users of a client a centralized template library. In the ⋮ menu of the client card, click **Configure a source template workspace**, then choose the **Source workspace** that holds your templates. The menu entry becomes **Library configured**: click it to edit or delete the library.

## Publish your partner page

The **Profile page** card lets you create or edit a profile page on the Dastra website to present your business. Click **Create**, then fill in the profile: logo, color, categories, languages, descriptions, website, phone, address and **Contact email**. This address receives the messages sent through the contact form of your page.

After you save, the page stays **Awaiting publication**: Dastra reviews and validates it before it goes live. Once it is published, a **View** link opens the page.
